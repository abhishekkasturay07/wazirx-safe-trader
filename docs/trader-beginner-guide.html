WazirX Trading Bot — Beginner Guide

Simple Hinglish explanation of BUY score, risk, exits, fees, code changes and every environment variable.

Current project code aur supplied Render configuration par based. API keys, passwords aur personal details intentionally include nahi kiye gaye.

Sabse zaroori baat: Bot profit guarantee nahi karta. Score 75 ka matlab “price zaroor badhega” ya “75% chance of profit” nahi hai. Iska matlab sirf programmed conditions mein se kam-se-kam 75 points true hain.

1. Bot simple language mein kya karta hai?

Bot WazirX ke INR crypto pairs ki 15-minute price candles dekhta hai. Har pair ko technical conditions se score deta hai. Achcha score milne par BUY limit order submit karta hai. Position khulne ke baad stop loss, target aur trailing stop se exit decide karta hai.

  1. WazirX se candles download hoti hain.
  2. Entry indicators ke liye sirf poori tarah close ho chuki candle use hoti hai.
  3. BTC ka overall trend check hota hai.
  4. Har coin ka score calculate hota hai.
  5. Configured threshold (Render par 75) ke candidates collect hote hain.
  6. Sab candidates ko score aur relative volume se rank kiya jata hai.
  7. Risk rules aur slots allow karein to highest-ranked candidate par BUY limit order submit hota hai.
  8. Open position har scan mein latest available price se check hoti hai.

Total maximum score = 100. Render par MIN_SIGNAL_SCORE=75 hai.

BUY banane wale kuch combinations

  • EMA 25 + RSI 20 + MACD 20 + BTC 15 = 80
  • EMA 25 + MACD 20 + Volume 20 + BTC 15 = 80
  • EMA 25 + RSI 20 + Volume 20 + BTC 15 = 80
  • EMA 25 + RSI 20 + MACD 20 + Volume 20 = 85, even if BTC filter false
  • All conditions = 100
75+ ke baad bhi guaranteed purchase nahi: daily-loss breaker clear ho, consecutive-loss limit clear ho, same coin active na ho, slot aur INR available ho, aur exchange limit order fill kare.

3. Indicators bahut simple language mein

EMA20 aur EMA50

EMA moving average hai jo recent prices ko zyada importance deta hai. EMA20 upar hona upward trend ka clue hai, guarantee nahi.

RSI

RSI momentum meter jaisa hai, generally 0–100. Bot 40–65 aur rising RSI chahta hai: momentum improve ho, par move bahut stretched na ho.

MACD cross

MACD short aur long momentum compare karta hai. Histogram zero ke upar cross kare to possible upward momentum change maana jata hai. Sideways market mein false signals aa sakte hain.

Volume

Volume batata hai kitna coin trade hua. Bot latest completed candle ka volume previous 20 completed candles ke average se compare karta hai. Ratio 1.1 ka matlab average se 10% zyada.

BTC filter

Altcoins BTC se influence ho sakte hain, isliye BTC trend ko 15 points milte hain. Ye full protection nahi; multiple altcoins ek saath gir sakte hain.

5. Stop loss, target aur trailing stop

STOP_LOSS_PERCENT=2
TARGET_PERCENT=7
TRAILING_STOP_PERCENT=4

Agar entry price Rs 100 ho:

  • Initial stop approximately Rs 98.
  • Target approximately Rs 107.
  • Trailing stop highest observed scan price se 4% neeche.

Starting mein 2% initial stop tighter hai. 4% trailing stop tab initial stop se upar aata hai jab observed price entry se roughly 2.08% upar chala jaye.

Example: highest observed price Rs 105 hua to trailing level Rs 100.80. Future scan mein price Rs 100.80 ya neeche dikhe to exit trigger ho sakta hai.

Current limitation: Exit continuous exchange-native stop nahi hai. Bot scan ke waqt sampled price check karke ordinary limit sell submit karta hai. Beech ka movement miss ho sakta hai aur fast fall mein sell unfilled reh sakti hai.

6. Fees aur real profit

TRADING_FEE_PERCENT per-side fee hai; entry aur exit dono par lag sakti hai. 0.2% per side par 7% nominal target roughly +6.57% net aur 2% stop roughly -2.39% net hota hai, spread/slippage se pehle.

Actual WazirX fee account plan/tier se different ho sakti hai. Completed fill ki fee dekhkar variable set karo. TDS cash flow, GST, spread, slippage aur latency current backtest mein fully model nahi hain.

10. Kya ab profit hoga?

Honest answer: abhi pata nahi. Changes execution consistency aur safety improve karte hain; profitable edge prove nahi karte.

Profitability ke liye actual closed trades se win rate, average win/loss, profit factor, expectancy, fees, slippage, maximum drawdown aur symbol-wise performance chahiye.

Beginner rule: Loss recover karne ke liye position double, leverage, futures, martingale ya averaging down mat use karo. Paper data collect karo aur ek-ek change test karo.
